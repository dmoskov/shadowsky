## 5. Description (4000 char max)

```
Asphodel is a powerful, privacy-first client for Bluesky and the AT Protocol. Designed for people who want more from decentralized social media.

SKYDECK — MULTI-COLUMN DASHBOARD
See everything at once. Add customizable columns for your timeline, notifications, messages, bookmarks, trending topics, and custom feeds. Swipe between columns on mobile or view them side by side on tablet.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Asphodel's dual-storage system lets you decide where your data lives. Keep bookmarks, drafts, and columns stored locally on your device for maximum privacy — or sync them to your Bluesky PDS so they follow you everywhere.

REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS
Stay connected with instant WebSocket-powered notifications via Jetstream. Smart aggregation groups likes, reposts, and follows together. Filter by type, see who interacts with you most, and browse up to 4 weeks of notification history.

ADVANCED SEARCH
Find anything with powerful search filters: phrases, hashtags, specific users, mentions, date ranges, domains, languages, and media types. Search your bookmarks too.

COMPOSE WITH POWER
Write posts and threads with a full-featured composer. Add images with 20+ filters and cropping tools. Generate alt text with AI. Search for GIFs. Save drafts that auto-sync. Schedule posts for later.

ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS
Track your engagement over time. See your top interactions, activity patterns, and posting trends with visual charts and breakdowns.

DIRECT MESSAGES
Full messaging support with real-time updates, conversation search, and message reactions.

CONTENT MODERATION
Mute users and threads, block accounts, and manage AT Protocol content labels. Customize your content filtering to see only what you want.

FEED DISCOVERY
Explore and subscribe to community-created feeds. Add any feed as a SkyDeck column for a truly personalized experience.

THREAD VISUALIZATION
Navigate complex conversations with a visual thread minimap. Color-coded replies help you follow who said what.

BUILT ON OPEN STANDARDS
Asphodel is built entirely on the AT Protocol — the same open, decentralized protocol that powers Bluesky. Connect to any compatible PDS. Your account, your data, your rules.

WORKS OFFLINE
Browse cached posts, notifications, and bookmarks without an internet connection. Queued posts send automatically when you reconnect.

ACCESSIBLE BY DESIGN
Full keyboard navigation, screen reader support, high-contrast mode, and reduced-motion preferences. Meets WCAG accessibility guidelines.

FREE AND AD-FREE
No ads. No tracking. No premium tiers. Just a great Bluesky experience.
```

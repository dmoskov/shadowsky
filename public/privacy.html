Asphodel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Asphodel is a third-party client for the Bluesky social network, built on the AT Protocol. We are committed to being transparent about how the app handles your information.

Data we do not collect

Asphodel does not operate a server or Personal Data Server (PDS). We do not collect, store, or process your posts, messages, profile information, or social graph. All of that data lives on your PDS (typically hosted by Bluesky at bsky.social) and is accessed directly by the app on your device.

Authentication

When you sign in, your credentials are sent directly to your PDS. Your session tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We never see or store your password.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

  • Session tokens for authenticated access to your PDS
  • Your app preferences (theme, layout, notification settings, etc.)
  • Cached feed content for offline viewing
  • Search history
  • Draft posts

All local data can be cleared by signing out or deleting the app.

Crash reporting

We use Sentry to collect crash reports and performance data. This includes device type, OS version, app version, and stack traces when errors occur. Crash reports do not contain your posts, messages, or personal information. You can disable crash reporting in the app settings.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is registered with Expo's notification service to deliver alerts. We do not use push tokens for any other purpose.

GIF search

GIF search runs through Bluesky's own GIF proxy at gifs.bsky.app, which is backed by KLIPY. Your searches go to Bluesky's proxy rather than directly to a GIF provider, so the provider does not receive your IP address or device information from the app. GIFs in older posts may still be delivered through the same proxy's legacy Tenor path.

Third-party services

  • Bluesky / AT Protocol — All social features, and the GIF proxy described above. See Bluesky's privacy policy.
  • Sentry — Crash reporting. See Sentry's privacy policy.
  • Expo — Push notifications and OTA updates. See Expo's privacy policy.

Camera and photo library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to take or select photos and videos for posts, messages, or your profile. Media is uploaded directly to your PDS. We do not access your camera or photos without your explicit action.

Biometric data

If you enable app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is used for authentication on your device. Biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by the operating system.

Children

Asphodel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated date at the top will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@shadowsky.io.
